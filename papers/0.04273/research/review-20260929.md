# Independent review record, 2026-09-29

Three referees reviewed [construction.md](construction.md) in fresh contexts,
with no author verdicts. Each was asked to find errors and to recompute
independently. Their scratch work is in the session scratchpad and was not
preserved in the repository. The findings and the resulting changes are
summarized here.

## Tower over Q(√241) (§3)

**Verdict: no mathematical error.**

The referee recomputed the following with independent code: a p-adic
Hilbert-symbol implementation, and GF(2) bitset elimination that reproduces
the manuscript's Q data exactly (16 masks, ranks 16/92/93, L₃=19, ad(c) ranks
6/6).

* Arithmetic of B (bnfcertify): h=1, unit of norm −1, dim V=8.
* Cup-product table, identical in all 36 entries, of rank 7 after dropping
  any single place.
* For each of the 8 places, the local relator initials from the stated
  conventions equal the matrix of local Hilbert symbols (the relation–cup
  identity). The 8 initials sum to zero.
* L₂=15 (the single dependency is the reciprocity sum); in degree 3 the
  brackets have rank 140, and 142 with the two cubics, so L₃=26 and |Q|=2⁴⁹.
* Retention of D, C₂² and C₄, and ad(c₁) ranks 7 and 8.
* The algebra facts behind s_D (dimensions 32,31,…,1,0; kernel at n=7).
* P_B(34/117) exactly, with P_B<0 only on about t∈[0.2755,0.3058].
  Infinitude rests on the single s_D subtraction, as in the manuscript over Q.

Fixed:

* The Kummer table's α₃ was off by the factor −1 relative to the scripts.
* θ is sharpened to 65535/131072, from the note's own b/d ≤ 2⁻¹⁶.

## Geometric transfer and margin (§5)

**Verdict: no error.** No step of `geo:mass`, `geo:transfer` or `fw:transfer`
uses a Galois structure over Q; every hypothesis was checked.

The referee reimplemented M_*(θ) independently in numpy/scipy: brute-force
local overlaps in Q₂ and Q₃, quadrature for J_C and J_D. The same code
reproduces the manuscript's published M_* = 5.336044e-6, finite sum and
slope. It matches geom241 to all printed digits: 1.767260336e-4 at δ=0.04273
and 9.06321470e-4 at δ=0.0427, with θ=65535/131072.

The Fourier condition only needs σμ ≥ log M + 2log5 + 2 ≈ 5.91 here, and
μ=17.87.

Fixed:

* Stale margins and "H≈11.3" in the note; H=15.69.
* geom241.py now asserts its Fourier and slope checks and runs at 80 digits.
  The results are identical to 40 and to 140 digits.
* The shell files for δ=0.0426 and 0.0428 were added.
