\section*{Appendix: The Selected Prime Certificate}

The following table lists the complete selected prime set $S$ of section
\ref{sec:certificate}. For each $p\in S$ it records the weight $k(p)$,
the ramification index $e(p)$ of $p$ in the tower fields $F$, and the
status of $p$: dyadic ($p=2$), ramified ($p\in T$), or split or inert in
$Q_{0}=\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{D})$ according to the Kronecker symbol
$\left(\frac{D}{p}\right)$. The witness column certifies admissibility
(\ref{eq:admissible}): it is blank when $p\equiv1\pmod4$, and otherwise
it lists a prime $q\in T$ with $\left(\frac{q}{p}\right)=-1$, while in
the dyadic row the entry $5$ records the unramified dyadic square class
of (\ref{eq:Aq}).
