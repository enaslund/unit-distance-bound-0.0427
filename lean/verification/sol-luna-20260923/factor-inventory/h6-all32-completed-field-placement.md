# H6 mask-1586 quartics and the completed field

Date: 2026-09-23 UTC. Source review only; no Lean or numerical job was run.

## Field names and existing results

There are two fields whose names must be kept distinct:

* `CanonicalRetained.Carrier` is the independently specified field generated
  by seven genus roots and twelve retained roots. By
  `CanonicalRetained.degree` and `ArithmeticRetained.retainedField_degree`,
  its rational degree is `524288 = 2^19`; `CanonicalRetained.equiv` identifies
  it with `ArithmeticRetained.RetainedField`.
* `ArithmeticCompleted.CompletedField` is the seven-radical completed field
  over the genus field. Its rational degree is `16384 = 2^14`, not 524288.
  `ArithmeticCompleted.completedEmbedding` already embeds it in the full
  `RetainedField`, and `RetainedFixedZetaReduction.completedRetained_degree`
  gives relative degree 32.

The existing completed-field factor inventory is explicit about its current
scope: 128 genus-character factors plus one aggregate relative Euler factor
for `CompletedField/GenusField`. It does not assign that last factor to
individual manuscript rows (`ZetaLunaFactorInventoryRun20260923.lean`).

The H6 source data list the 32 twists as
`±1, ±2, ±3, ±5, ±6, ±10, ±11, ±13, ±15, ±22, ±26, ±30, ±55, ±65, ±110,
±130`. Their prime support is contained in `{2,3,5,11,13}`, all among the
seven genus radicands `−1,2,3,5,7,11,13`. Thus no listed `D` has a rational
squareclass outside the genus field: each `D` has a square root in
`ArithmeticChosenGenus.GenusField`. This is a direct squareclass observation;
the repository has not yet encoded the 32-entry mask assignment as a Lean
theorem.

For `D = -1`,

## Concrete placement witness suggested by the tables

The tables point to a specific common quadratic extension over the genus
field. Catalog row 9 in `NormExtensionCatalog.catalog` has
`(A,B,x,y,z)=(-35,429,1,7,2)` and `masksA 9 = 25`, so its radicand is
`catalogAlpha 9 = 1 + 7s` with `s^2 = -35`. The retained-word list contains
`512 = 2^9` at index 2, hence
`retainedRadicand 2 = wordRadicand 512 = catalogAlpha 9`.

In the genus field, let `r^2=770` (available as the product of the actual
roots of `2,5,7,11`) and let `s^2=-35`. A direct field calculation gives

```text
(17 + 2s)/(1 + 7s) = (13 - 3s)/44
                    = (r/44 - 3s/(2r))^2.
```

Therefore the H6 radicand `η=17+2s` and retained word 512 have the same
squareclass over the genus field. This identity has not been formalized in
Lean. It implies that a root of word 512 in `RetainedField` would give a root
of `η` there.

There is also a short raw-word identity in the existing completed inventory:

```text
512 = 6220 XOR 2 XOR 32768 XOR 39508 XOR 26.
```

The four words `6220, 2, 32768, 39508` are completed words at indices 2–5;
`wordRadicand 26` is proved square in the genus field by
`ArithmeticCatalog.wordRadicand_26_isSquare`. This suggests that word 512 is
already a squareclass in `CompletedField`, not only in the 19-radical retained
field. The repository's `CatalogCompletedContainmentData` proves the opposite
direction needed for its existing embedding—completed words as combinations
of retained words and 26—but does not expose this reverse H6-specific
certificate or the resulting root in `CompletedField`. The displayed XOR
identity was checked as integer arithmetic in this source review only.

Because every listed `D` is a square in the genus field, all 32 composita
`GenusField(√(Dη))` would then be the same quadratic extension of the genus
field. Thus no twist lies outside the tower at the rational squareclass
level, and the table identities give a concrete route to place all 32 quartic
fields in `CompletedField`. This is a source-level mathematical inference,
not an existing Lean embedding theorem.

## Missing Lean bridge and remaining factor gap

The smallest useful missing statements are:

1. A finite H6 twist-mask certificate: each listed `D` is the product of a
   specified subset of the actual genus radicands, with an actual square root
   in `GenusField`.
2. An H6 class identity in `GenusField`: `η` differs from
   `ArithmeticRetained.retainedRadicand 2` by the displayed square. A second
   finite certificate should show the raw-word-512 class is generated by
   completed words 2–5 and word 26, then use the existing completed tower
   roots to prove `∃x : CompletedField, x^2 = algebraMap GenusField
   CompletedField η`.
3. A generic root-to-field-map lemma, instantiated with `s` and
   `sqrt(Dη)`, giving an injective `ℚ`-algebra map from each abstract quartic
   `K_D` into `CompletedField` (or a single lemma over the finite twist type).
4. A separate factor-inventory theorem identifying the aggregate
   `CompletedField/GenusField` Euler factor with the product of the intended
   H6 Artin/Hecke factors. Embeddings alone do not prove this equality. The
   source-level good-prime formula covers primes outside the common
   exceptional set; separate paper local calculations and a fresh maximal-order
   PARI replay cover the seven listed bad factors for all 32 rows. Turning
   those local calculations into Lean theorems, the aggregate factor identity,
   and the analytic data remains open.
