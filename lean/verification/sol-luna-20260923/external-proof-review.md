# Independent audit: external numerical bridge to (H)

## Scope and finding

I traced the manuscript's finite-field factor estimate, exceptional Euler
restoration, two finite prime savings, and logarithmic-derivative debit to
the fixed-field expression used as (H). I checked the formulas and their
directions against `publication/unit-distance-1.0418235/sections/analytic.tex`
and the included numerical provenance notes. I did not rerun the large
factor evaluation or the prime sieve.

I found no concrete sign, degree-normalization, or disjoint-support error in
this bridge. Conditional on the stated factor, field, and census inputs, the
displayed inequalities have the direction needed to upper-bound the actual
fixed-field (H). The calculation is a computer-assisted certificate, not a
Lean proof of (H); some stored analytic moments and the full census are not
regenerated by the current replay.

## Formula trace

Set `M` to the degree `2^19 = 524288` field in the manuscript,
`N ⊂ M` to its degree `2^14 = 16384` subfield, `S'={2,3,5,7,11,13,17}`,
and `σ=12001/12000`. The factorization in `analytic.tex:201–225` defines

\[
Y(\sigma)=\frac{\log\zeta_N^{S'}(\sigma)}{16384}
=\frac1{16384}\sum_\rho \dim(\rho)\log|L^{S'}(\sigma,\rho)|.
\]

The degree-weighted sum counts both members of conjugate pairs. The regular
representation degree check is
`128 + 256·4 + 456·16 + 124·64 = 16384` (`:207–225`). Thus this is the
normalized logarithm of the zeta function with exactly the `S'` factors
removed, rather than an unweighted factor sum.

For a rational prime with local indices `(e,f)` in a Galois field, its
normalized logarithmic contribution is

\[
\Phi_{p,s}(e,f)=\frac{-\log(1-p^{-fs})}{ef}.
\]

Both increasing `e` and increasing `f` decrease this positive term
(`analytic.tex:60–68`). Since local indices multiply in a tower, the
normalized contribution for `M` is at most that for `N` outside `S'`.
Restoring the seven actual `M` factors at `S'` gives

\[
B_D(\sigma)=-\frac{\log(1-2^{-4\sigma})}{32}
-\sum_{p=3,5}\frac{\log(1-p^{-2\sigma})}{4}
-\sum_{p=7,11,13}\frac{\log(1-p^{-4\sigma})}{8}
-\frac{\log(1-17^{-4\sigma})}{4},
\]

which uses `ef=32,4,8,4`, respectively (`:747–757`). I found these
denominators consistent with the stated local indices.

The two saving formulas also have the right signs. On a `P4` prime, the
manuscript's tests give `e=1`, `f_N=2`, and `f_M=4`. With `x=p^{-σ}`, the
drop from `N` to `M` is

\[
\tfrac12[-\log(1-x^2)]-\tfrac14[-\log(1-x^4)]
=\tfrac14\log\frac{1+x^2}{1-x^2}>0,
\]

the summand used for `S4` (`:759–772`). On a census prime, splitting in `N`
and nonsplitting in the full central field give `f_N=1`, `f_M=2`, so the
drop is

\[
-\log(1-x)+\tfrac12\log(1-x^2)
=\operatorname{atanh}(x)>0.
\]

Those primes have zero genus vector while `P4` primes have nonzero genus
vector, so the supports are disjoint (`:774–784`). The finite sums therefore
subtract from the upper bound without overlap:

\[
\frac{\log\zeta_M(\sigma)}{524288}
\leq Y(\sigma)+B_D(\sigma)-S4-S_{\rm cen}.
\]

The census's outward-bin estimate uses
`x ≤ atanh(x) ≤ x/(1-x²)` and exact integer counts/floors (`:786–846`).
This supports a lower bound for the finite saving only; the text explicitly
claims no saving from primes above `10^12`.

For the derivative, the ideal Euler product gives

\[
-\frac1{524288}\frac{\zeta'_M}{\zeta_M}(2)
=\sum_p\frac{\log p}{e_M(p)(p^{2f_M(p)}-1)}\geq0.
\]

The numerator `log p` and denominator `e(p^{2f}-1)` follow by summing the
`524288/(ef)` prime ideals of norm `p^f`, each contributing
`f log(p)/(p^{2f}-1)`, and dividing by the degree. Thus the derivative has
the positive sign in (H)'s debit. Lower bounds on `e,f` give upper bounds
on these terms. Above `10^4`, replacing both indices by one and summing over
all integers gives the stated convergent tail. This agrees with
`analytic.tex:908–937` and the logarithmic-derivative identity in
`UnitDistance/PrimeDebitLogDerivative.lean:20–62`.

Writing `R` for that debit bound, the fixed-field expression is bounded by

\[
H_M\leq Y+B_D-S4-S_{\rm cen}
+\frac1{12000}\left(\frac{\ell-\gamma-\log(4\pi)}4+R\right),
\]

exactly as in `analytic.tex:854–903`. The sign of `-logDeriv` agrees with
the Lean equality identifying `normalizedPrimeDebit` with minus the real
logarithmic derivative divided by degree.

The five printed rational allowances add to
`0.04216181893948094953138458`, below the selected Lean ceiling
`0.042165819` by `0.00000400006051905046861542`. The number in the manuscript
is therefore a conservative *upper-bound certificate endpoint*, not a
computed value or narrow enclosure of the true `H_M`.

## Validation boundary and remaining inputs

The fixed-field bridge depends on the manuscript's arithmetic identifications:
the `N ⊂ M` inclusion; the local indices at `S'`; the 45 `P4` local
conditions; and the implication from the central-form predicates to
`f_M=2` or `4`. The manuscript states these through its retained-field
proposition and radicand/local calculations (`sections/retained-field.tex`
and `analytic.tex:854–884`). The included supplemental scripts reconstruct
field masks, norm identities, and finite lists, but their own scope records
say the Kummer/local-index interpretation remains a mathematical input
(`verification/sol-luna-20260922/submission-supplement.md:13–16`). I did
not re-prove that field-theoretic identification here, so my positive
conclusion is conditional on it.

The analytic `Y` endpoint depends on all 836 factor bounds and their stored
moment data. The September 22 replay re-evaluated every factor bound but
hash-checked, rather than regenerated, the full moment tables and the
`10^12` census (`verification/external-zeta-20260922/README.md:51–65`).
Supplemental checks regenerate selected census ranges and selected
coefficient rows, while listing the full-suffix and all-row limitations
(`submission-supplement.md:17–28`). I treated these as scope limitations,
not as evidence of a sign error.

The tight standalone numerical debit uses a 50-digit lower input for Euler's
constant. The supplemental rational debit checker also records a second
route using the Lean-proved coarse bound `γ ≥ 5767/10000`; with that weaker
bound, its sum with the other four allowances still lies below `0.042165819`
by more than `0.0000039893` (`submission-supplement.md:16`). Thus the
high-precision Euler-constant input is not needed for the relaxed selected
ceiling, assuming the other four allowances.

This audit verifies formula direction and normalization by source inspection;
it does not establish the full external numerical computation, independently
reconstruct the census, prove all Hecke/Artin identifications, or discharge
(H) in Lean. No expensive run was performed. I changed no repository file
except this report.
