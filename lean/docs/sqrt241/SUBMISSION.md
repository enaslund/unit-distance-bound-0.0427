## The theorem

For the ordinary Euclidean plane (`ℂ`), there is a sequence of finite sets `U_j`
with `|U_j| → ∞` and `unitPairs(U_j) / |U_j|^(20863/20000) → ∞`, where
`unitPairs` counts unordered pairs at distance one. It is a sequence statement;
nothing is asserted at every sufficiently large cardinality.

## The one hypothesis H_W

Let `E` be the subfield of `AlgebraicClosure ℚ` generated by a square root of 241 and square
roots of the eight elements `a + b√241` of `B = ℚ(√241)`

| k | radicand | norm |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | −1 | 1 |
| 1 | ε = −71011068 + 4574225√241 | −1 |
| 2 | π₂ = (−6101 − 393√241)/2 | −2 |
| 3 | π₂′ = (6101 − 393√241)/2 | −2 |
| 4 | π₃ = 31 − 2√241 | −3 |
| 5 | π₃′ = 31 + 2√241 | −3 |
| 6 | π₅ = 326 − 21√241 | −5 |
| 7 | π₅′ = 326 + 21√241 | −5 |

These are a basis of the S-units of B modulo squares, S = {2, 3, 5}, and `E` is the
genus field of the 2-tower of B unramified outside 2, 3, 5 (and ∞), of degree 512. Let
`E_W = CanonicalWide.Carrier` be the subfield generated by `E` and square roots of the four
elements `β_i = CanonicalWide.wideRadicand i` of `E`, written in the Challenge in terms of
`√241`, `√a_i = rootA i` and `√b_i = rootB i` (products of the eight square roots). Each
`B(√a_i, √b_i, √β_i)` is the dihedral field of degree 8 of the form 24, 20, 17, 7 of
`papers/0.043171/certificates/dihedral/d4all27.json`; the four forms span the space `W''` of
the manuscript, so `E_W` is its field `E_{W''}`, of degree 8192 (proved:
`Wide.finrank_field`). It is Galois over `B` but not over `ℚ`. H_W is

    log(Re ζ_{E_W}(1 + 1/4411))/8192
      + (1/4411)·((ℓ − γ − log 4π)/4 − Re((ζ_{E_W}′/ζ_{E_W})(2))/8192) < 50969/1000000,

with `ℓ = (9/4) log 2 + (1/2) log 3615`.

**H_W is not proved in Lean.** It is an explicit hypothesis, not an axiom.

**External evidence.** `papers/0.043171/certificates/dihedral/h_w_receipt.py` writes
`h_w_receipt.json` and bounds the left side by **0.0509171473**, which is **5.2·10⁻⁵** below
the threshold. It combines

* `(1/8192) log ζ_{E_W}(4412/4411)`, from the factorization of `ζ_{E_W}` into `ζ_E`, the squares
  of 448 Hecke L-functions of degree 4 and the fourth powers of 128 of degree 8, and their
  certified values at `4412/4411` (Sections 5 and 6 of the manuscript);
* `−(ζ_{E_W}′/ζ_{E_W})(2)/8192`, bounded above from the types of `E` above 2, 3, 5, the exact
  residue degrees in `E_W/B` of the unramified primes of nonzero Frobenius vector and the bound
  `f ≥ 1` at the others, up to norm `10⁶`, and an explicit tail.
