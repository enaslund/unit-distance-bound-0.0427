## The theorem

For the ordinary Euclidean plane (`ℂ`), there is a sequence of finite sets `U_j`
with `|U_j| → ∞` and `unitPairs(U_j) / |U_j|^(10427/10000) → ∞`, where
`unitPairs` counts unordered pairs at distance one. It is a sequence statement;
nothing is asserted at every sufficiently large cardinality.

## The one hypothesis H

Let `E` be `CanonicalGenus.Carrier`: the subfield of `AlgebraicClosure ℚ`
generated by a square root of 241 and square roots of the eight elements
`a + b√241` of `B = ℚ(√241)`

| k | radicand | norm |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | −1 | 1 |
| 1 | ε = −71011068 + 4574225√241 | −1 |
| 2 | π₂ = (−6101 − 393√241)/2 | −2 |
| 3 | π₂′ = (6101 − 393√241)/2 | −2 |
| 4 | π₃ = 31 − 2√241 | −3 |
| 5 | π₃′ = 31 + 2√241 | −3 |
| 6 | π₅ = 326 − 21√241 | −5 |
| 7 | π₅′ = 326 + 21√241 | −5 |

These are a basis of the S-units of B modulo squares, S = {2, 3, 5}. So `E` is
the genus field of the 2-tower of B unramified outside 2, 3, 5 (and ∞), of
degree 512 (proved: `Genus.finrank_carrier`). H is

    log(Re ζ_E(1 + 1/300))/512
      + (1/300)·((ℓ − γ − log 4π)/4 − Re((ζ_E′/ζ_E)(2))/512) < 852/10000,

with `ℓ = (9/4) log 2 + (1/2) log 3615`. The same fixed-base form is used by
`ChallengeZeta.lean`.

**H is not proved in Lean.** It is an explicit hypothesis, not an axiom.

**External evidence.** `papers/0.04273/certificates/h241_receipt.py`
writes `h241_receipt.json` and bounds the left side by **0.0848335193**, which is
**0.000366** below the ceiling. It combines two ingredients:

* `(1/512) log ζ_E(301/300) ∈ [0.08264460177, 0.08264460807]`, from `afe241.py`.
  E is abelian over B, so ζ_E = ζ_B·∏ L(s, χ) over the 255 quadratic Hecke
  characters of E/B. The enclosure bounds each L-value by the manuscript's
  certified degree-two approximate functional equation. PARI's `lfun` gives
  0.0826446049.
* `−(ζ_E′/ζ_E)(2)/512 ≤ 0.0197321538`, from the Euler product over the primes of
  B of norm ≤ 10⁶ with the exact local types of E, plus an explicit tail bound.

An earlier independent analytic review of `afe241` found no error
(`papers/0.04273/research/review-20260929.md`).
