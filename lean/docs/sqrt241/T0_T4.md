Everything is a literal retype of the ℚ files `UnitDistance/{FinitePExtension, …}.lean`
with `ℚ ↦ F` (explicit argument, `F : Type`, `[Field F] [NumberField F]`) and

### Mathematics as formalized (for review)

* Stage bound. For a stage `E` of `Ω_B/B` (finite Galois 2-extension unramified outside
  `S`), `f` = S-place localization of `H²(Gal(E/B), μ₂)` (image `≤ 2^6`, upstream) and
  `g` = field-unit Kummer map. `g(ker f)` lies in the finite-place detection kernel of
  `H²(Gal(E/B), Eˣ)`, which has `≤ 2` elements, so `|g(A)| ≤ 2 · 2^6`. The kernel of `g`
  dies under inflation to `Gal(Ω_B/B)` (stage-kernel theorem), giving `|infl. image| ≤ 2^7`
  and `dim H² ≤ 7` by `finiteDimensional_and_finrank_degree_two_le_of_inflationRange_natCard`.
* Detection kernel. Quadratic cyclic case `L/K`, `K` with two infinite places: a scalar
  representative `b` of a class vanishing at all finite places is a local norm there; the
  class is determined by the Artin symbol of `b` at one real place `v₀` (Artin product
  formula + cyclic Hasse), so the kernel embeds in `Gal(L/K)` of order 2. General `L/B`:
  inflate to `N = L(i)`, restrict to `Gal(N/B(i))` (`B(i)` totally complex, detection
  holds), tower exactness puts the class in the inflation image of the `B(i)/B` kernel.
* Character with prescribed inertia (route R). Correct the family on the six places of `S`
  by `a·γ₋₁ + b·γ_ε`; reciprocity sums over `S` read off at the real places (global
  reciprocity for principal units; positive reals are squares). With
  `c(r) = Σ_{v∈S'∪S} ⟨χ_v, r⟩`: `b = c(−1)`, `a = c(ε) + c(−1)`.
